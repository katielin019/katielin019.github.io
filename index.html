Katie Lin is a product designer and engineer.

I'm interested in alternative models of education, non-games and sandbox environments, social models of computing, design systems, the history of technology, and personal canons.

Quilt was an audio social network focused on self-care.

It began as a community platform that facilitated IRL conversations. Quilt's 1.0 audio app was a direct response to the COVID-19 pandemic. I joined Quilt in October 2020 as a product marketer focused on GTM strategy. As with many startups, I wore several hats, and within 2 months, I ended up working as a full-fledged product designer.
