HyprRun

Fall 2022

with Zahir Saiyed and Emily Ngo

HyprRun is a mobile app built to be your personal running companion. As you run, the app queues up the best tracks to match your current 'vibe' and your individual music taste.

View our final presentation

Tools: Xcode, Figma, Slack, Git/GitHub

Dependencies: CoreData, CoreML, CoreLocation, Spotify Web API, Spotify iOS SDK, SwiftUI, UIKit, MapKit, Combine
