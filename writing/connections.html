Observations from playing the NYT Connections

My girlfriend and I have been playing the NYT Connections for several months now. We don't play every single day, but we try to do it together as often as we can. Connections is a daily word puzzle developed by the NYT Games team; the beta version was released on June 12, 2023. It's been noted that it is conceptually similar to a British TV quiz show called Only Connect.

For context, my girlfriend is autistic. I have ADHD. Together, we have AuDHD. This combo can be quite lethal, but I think we work really well together, in part due to our mutual neurodiversity. The Connections game illuminates, for me, a lot of interesting patterns and insights about the ways in which we respectively think and solve problems.
